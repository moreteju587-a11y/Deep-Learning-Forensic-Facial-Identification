Workflow

Three steps from description to lead

No sketch artist required. Just a witness, a microphone, and the case moves forward.

1

Describe by voice

The witness answers guided prompts about facial features. Speech is transcribed and normalized in real time.

2

AI generates the face

Collected features are converted into a prompt and rendered into a realistic composite face by a generative model.

3

Match against records

The generated face is encoded and compared to the criminal database, returning the closest match with a confidence score.
